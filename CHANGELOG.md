# Changes

## 2026-11-13

- Items: Lightweave Embroidery procs only from magic spells and periodic damage; melee and ranged attacks, physical spells, totem damage and Saronite Bombs no longer proc it.
- Shaman: Glyph of Totem of Wrath adds spell power only with the Totem of Wrath talent.

## 2026-11-08

- Melee auto-attacks wait during a spell's cast time and land when the cast ends (players, pets, bosses).
- Pets: Spirit Wolves and the Shadowfiend keep their owner's inherited stats after an iteration that ended while they were out.
- Shaman: Spirit Wolves get only Bloodlust and Icy Talons or Windfury Totem melee haste from raid, party and individual buffs; base attack power 311 (was 642).
- Shaman: Greater Fire Elemental swings every 2.1 s, its spells trigger no global cooldown, Fire Blast no longer restarts its swing timer; damage per hit set to the Whitemane logs (melee x0.929, Fire Blast x1.023, Fire Nova x1.034, Fire Shield x0.977).
- Items: Flame Cap's fire damage procs on every landed melee and ranged auto attack (was 18.5% of all melee and ranged hits), extra auto attacks (Hand of Justice) included; special attacks (Raptor Strike and Rune Strike too) and triggered extra shots (Wild Quiver) do not proc it.
- Items: Sundial of the Exiled also procs from Flametongue Attack, Lightning Shield orbs and other effects (Flamecap Fire, totem drops, explosives, warlock curses).
- Hodir (tank sims): Flash Freeze takes effect when its cast ends; campfires go out 0-4 s later and burn again 15-19 s after the cast ends; storm clouds due during the cast or a few seconds after it come 3-7 s after it ends.

## 2026-11-03

- Heroic Presence reaches the whole raid, including pets and guardians.
- Scroll of Protection no longer adds armor on top of Mark of the Wild.
- Balance: the highest ranks of Starfire and Wrath deal 12% more base damage and have a 12% higher base spell power coefficient.
- Hunter: with Animal Handler, pet special-ability crits have a 50% or 100% chance to restore 1% of your mana.
- Paladin: Fanaticism gives auto-attacks a 20% chance to reset the cooldown of Divine Storm.
- Priest: new Glyph of Quick Pain: haste shortens the time between Shadow Word: Pain's ticks.
- Shaman: Fire Nova can only be cast while a fire totem or the Fire Elemental Totem is active.
- Shaman: Totem Remaining Time no longer carries a totem over from the previous iteration.
- Warlock: the instant Shadow Bolt of Shadow Trance uses it up also while Backdraft is active.
- Warrior: Sweeping Strikes affects the next 10 melee attacks.
- Warrior: Improved Mortal Strike increases Mortal Strike damage by 12, 23 or 35% and no longer shortens its cooldown.
- Items: Stormcoil casts Chain Lightning at 2.075 procs per minute with a 20 second cooldown.

## 2026-10-30

- Pets: warlock demons, the Water Elemental, hunter pets, the Raise Dead ghoul and the Shadowfiend choose their next spell only every 0.8 seconds.
- Judgement of Wisdom no longer procs from triggered spells such as Windfury, Stormstrike, Judgement damage, Arcane Missiles bolts and Starfall stars.
- The first iteration of every sim, the one the log shows, now calculates damage like all other iterations.
- Death Knight: Blood Boil deals its bonus damage to targets with any Death Knight's diseases; in the individual sim the Ebon Plaguebringer debuff counts as another Death Knight's.
- Death Knight: Threat of Thassarian off-hand strikes get the full flat damage bonus.
- Death Knight: Army of the Dead ghouls use Claw every 4 to 5.3 seconds.
- Feral: a Clearcasting Ferocious Bite spends up to 30 extra energy; the rotation no longer bites on Clearcasting.
- Balance: treants swing every 1.5 seconds and gain 75% of spell power as attack power.
- Hunter: pet focus abilities have a 1.5 second global cooldown.
- Hunter: Focused Fire adds its critical strike chance to every pet special attack during Kill Command.
- Mage: Arcane Potency is used up by the first Arcane Missiles bolt that hits and lasts through a Blizzard channel.
- Mage: Mirror Image inherits spell hit rating without the Precision talent.
- Paladin: Ardent Defender takes effect after other absorbs.
- Shaman: the Greater Fire Elemental casts Fire Nova every 8 to 10 seconds, each one followed by Fire Blast.
- Warrior: an Execute that uses Sudden Death can proc a new one.
- Warrior: Sweeping Strikes copies Execute as a normalized weapon hit and no longer copies Whirlwind or Bladestorm.
- Warrior: Thunder Clap gains no critical strike chance from Poleaxe Specialization.
- Warrior: with Glyph of Sunder Armor, Devastate sunders a second target once the first has 5 stacks.

## 2026-10-25

- Feral: a Clearcasting Ferocious Bite costs no energy and converts the full 30 extra energy; the rotation bites on Clearcasting.
- Enhancement: Greater Fire Elemental casts Fire Blast 57984 and Fire Nova 12470 with the Whitemane timing.
- Items: 895 items of the Whitemane client of 2026-10-15; 15 items updated.
- Upgrade slider updates the item list while dragged.
