# wowsim-rebuffed

World of Warcraft 3.3.5a simulations for the Whitemane Rebuffed server.
A fork of wowsims/wotlk.

## Built on oomshroom's work

This fork stands on the work of **oomshroom**: [oomshroom/wowsims335](https://github.com/oomshroom/wowsims335), the simulator at [oomshroom.github.io/wotlk](https://oomshroom.github.io/wotlk/).
oomshroom brought wowsims to 3.3.5 items and proc effects and built in the changes of the earlier Whitemane and of Stormforge:

- Flametongue without an internal cooldown (by Tobi)
- the Mjolnir Runestone gem suggestion (by Diandriss)
- Armata Strigoi, proc chance by Poli93
- the Whitemane legendaries Sulfuras, Warglaives, Thunderfury, Scythe of the Cat God and Atiesh

Their 19 commits are part of this branch with their original authors. Each names the commit it came from.

The simulator itself is [wowsims/wotlk](https://github.com/wowsims/wotlk) (MIT License, wowsims team).
